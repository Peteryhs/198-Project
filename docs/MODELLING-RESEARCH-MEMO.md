# Modelling research memo — 2026-11-07

**Position:** start with two small linear heads on accelerometer repetition features, evaluate only the labelled series verdicts, and withhold error explanations until independently annotated repetitions exist. This is a feasibility baseline, not a clinically validated real-time monitor.

All links below were opened as full text or primary documentation on 2026-11-07, including by the research subagents. Published results are attributed quotations of evidence, not measurements from our experiments. A universal 2024–2026 rehabilitation “state of the art” is not established: modalities, labels and splits differ.

| Evidence | What it establishes; limits for Tally |
| --- | --- |
| Li et al., **2026 preprint**, [WS-IMUBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01850) | Studies sequence labels and multiple-instance temporal localization on wearable activity data. Activity presence is not repetition correctness. Latent instance scores do not supply missing quality/error ground truth. Full method and evaluation opened. |
| Bock et al., **2024**, [Temporal Action Localization for Inertial-based HAR](https://kristofvl.github.io/usi/pdf/ubi_imwut24b.pdf) | Subject-independent temporal localization with start/end annotations; this is fully supervised activity localization, not a method that creates repetition-quality labels. It does not validate PHYTMO quality. Full PDF opened. |
| Karlov et al., **2024**, [supervised contrastive exercise assessment](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.02772v2) | A shared skeleton encoder uses exercise and correctness labels. Table 1 reports 99.80% on UI-PRMD with the RI descriptor and 99.20% on IRDS without it. Section 4.4 uses a 3:1 training/validation split; those headline numbers are **not established as LOSO**. Separate five-fold results appear in Table 4. Neither result is comparable to accelerometer-only LOSO. This is not a three-head error classifier. Full methods/tables opened. |
| Spilz et al., **2025 preprint, updated 2026**, [simulation-based IMU augmentation](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.24415v2) | Separates mixed-subject five-fold tests from held-subject LOSO. In the latter, deep-squat macro-F1 improves from .66±.39 to .87±.23 over 15 held-out people; hurdle-step changes .57±.32 to .60±.24. These use richer quaternion inputs and explicit repetition targets. Orientations, inverse kinematics and rule-defined labels do not reveal PHYTMO's unnamed errors. Full methods/results opened. |
| Spilz et al., **2025 online / 2026 volume**, [GAITEX](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12775516/) | Provides synchronized inertial/optical data and timestamped exercise variants. Its postprocessing section explicitly distinguishes instructed variants from independent movement-quality assessments. It is a dataset descriptor, not a universal accuracy benchmark. Full text opened. |
| Wang & Ma, **2022**, [PhysiQ](https://meiyima.github.io/papers/IMWUT22_PhysiQ.pdf) | An interpretable alternative: explicitly supervised range/stability/repetition targets from smartwatch movement, with leave-one-person-out evaluation. It illustrates the required target definitions; accelerometer acceleration is not a direct measurement of effort. Full PDF opened. |

**Multi-task implication (our inference):** exercise identity has filename labels; quality has series labels only. Propagating a series label to every repetition is a weak-label assumption. An error head has no supervised loss and is unidentified; masking its loss leaves it untrained. Architecture, attention and confidence cannot resolve that. Output `not enough evidence to say`, even when the binary score is confident. No direct validated three-head accelerometer-only PHYTMO model was found in the opened sources.

**Tooling:** [Arduino's R4 documentation](https://docs.arduino.cc/hardware/uno-r4-minima) and [Renesas core configuration](https://github.com/arduino/ArduinoCore-renesas/blob/main/boards.txt) support a direct C implementation. [TFLM's platform list](https://github.com/tensorflow/tflite-micro/blob/main/README.md), [Arduino wrapper](https://github.com/tensorflow/tflite-micro-arduino-examples), and [CMSIS-NN](https://arm-software.github.io/CMSIS_6/v6.0.0/NN/index.html) do not establish an unpatched, measured UNO R4 application. Use no inference framework for a linear model. See [embedded evidence](EMBEDDED-FEASIBILITY-NOTES.md) and [literature audit](MODELLING-LITERATURE-NOTES.md) for support qualifications and additional benchmarks.

**Research correction:** the existing findings' temporal-localization references are methodological leads, not evidence of labelled or accurate per-repetition PHYTMO correctness. Its old feature descriptions also disagree with `harness.window_features`: the actual order is statistic-major, each statistic covering x/y/z. Preserve old artifacts; make the new export's contract explicit.
