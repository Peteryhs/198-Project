## The three claims

You choose the wording and the visual. One claim per figure.

1. **Judging complete repetitions beats judging time slices.** 3 s windows 80.9%, per-repetition
   logistic regression 86.5%, per-repetition model 89.3%. Same sensors, same data.
2. **89.3% on people the model has never seen, from 1,450 nodes and 31 features.** The shipped
   107,528-node forest scored 88.3%, so the size cut cost nothing.
3. **Corrupting the sensor data barely moves the verdict.** Left side 92.36% real-only → 92.36%
   with synthetic corruption in training (paired 95% interval −1.31 to +1.32 points); right side
   89.87% → 90.20% (−0.67 to +1.62). Intervals that span zero mean no measurable change. Say what
   was corrupted: orientation up to 15°, ±0.02 g bias, gain error, gaussian noise, a 20 Hz one-pole
   low-pass, 0.001017 g ADC steps, clipping at 3 g, applied to training data only, held-out input
   untouched. If Peter wants the input-side version instead (frozen models, degraded test input,
   plus a rank-correlation column), that experiment does not exist yet and needs
   `scripts/augmentation_pipeline.py --generate-only` over four severity tiers.

## Creative freedom, with one test

- Any honest encoding: dot plot, slope chart, one large number with a reference mark, small
  multiples, paired numbers. Bars are not required.
- Dropping an axis is allowed when direct labels carry the values. One enormous "89.3%" with a thin
  reference line is a legitimate answer to claim 2.
- Test each figure at 50% zoom. If the claim is not readable there, the design is wrong.
- Keep roughly 60% of the canvas as data or as the single number. A third colour, a legend or a
  second y-axis means the figure is saying two things: split it or drop the weaker half.

## Minimal, without exception

- One short claim line per figure. No subtitle, no explanatory sentence inside the image.
- At most three text labels in the plot area, annotations included.
- Two colours: Ink plus either Clay or Oat. Never three series.
- No legend, no value label on every bar, no frames, no shadows, no gradients, no pie charts.
  Faint horizontal hairlines at 8% Ink are the only grid allowed.
- Five ticks maximum per axis, units in the axis label only.
- 1600 × 900 at 2×, Paper ground, at least 15% margin on every side.
