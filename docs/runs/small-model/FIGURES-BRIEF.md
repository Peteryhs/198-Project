# Figures brief — 198 form model progress

Tables in `docs/runs/small-model/figures/`, all generated by
`scripts/export_progress_tables.py` from the committed run outputs. Every number is a
leave-one-subject-out measurement over 30 volunteers unless the row says otherwise.

## The one-sentence story

A model that could not run on the board (2.5 MB, 107,528 nodes) became one that does
(13 KB of arrays, 1,450 nodes) without losing accuracy — the linked sketch is 96,164 B of
a 262,144 B flash budget.

## Chart 1 — accuracy vs model size (the main chart)

Data: `candidates-size-vs-accuracy.csv` (38 rows).

- x: `nodes`, log scale (125 → 114,860). y: `loso_accuracy_pct`.
- One series per `task` (rep, window), shape by `family`.
- Vertical line at **10,000 nodes** = the budget ceiling the training script enforces.
- Highlight the two `selected` points; annotate the two `reference` points (the shipped
  2.5 MB forests) as off to the right of the budget line.
- Read-off to label: rep 1,450 nodes = 89.33%, window 2,900 nodes = 81.08%.

## Chart 2 — progress ladder, overall

Data: `per-exercise.csv`, rows where `exercise = ALL`; plus 50% chance from `headline.csv`.

- Two panels (rep series, window rows), grouped bars: chance, logistic regression,
  reference RF (80 trees, depth 14), new small HGB.
- Bar labels are percentages, one decimal. The window floor is 49.85%, not exactly 50
  (window classes are slightly unbalanced) — label the line "chance, 49.9%".

## Chart 3 — per exercise, where the change lands

Data: `per-exercise.csv`, rows where `exercise != ALL`.

- Grouped bars per exercise (EAH, EFE, SQZ) with the small model against the reference RF.
- Print the `delta_vs_reference_pp` on each small-model bar.
- Say in the caption that the window model gives up 1.28 points on SQZ to gain 1.26 on EFE,
  and that the rep EAH loss is 10 series out of 464.

## Chart 4 — what fits in 262 kB

Data: `footprint.csv`.

- Horizontal stacked bar of the 262,144 B flash limit: empty sketch 38,984, both model
  objects 39,890, remainder 183,270.
- Second bar for RAM: 22,824 B used of 32,768 B.
- Only the flash total (96,164 B) is a linked measurement; the two model-object blocks are
  object sizes. Say that in the caption — `scope` column carries it.

## Chart 5 — why 3 s windows

Data: `window-length-sweep.csv` (from `docs/RESULTS.md`, rerun baselines).

- Line chart, x = window seconds (1, 2, 3), one line per model (logreg, rf, hgb).
- Shows the window length is a settled choice, not a knob still being turned.

## Caption rules

- Every figure: "LOSO over 30 volunteers, 1,368 series / 29,871 windows, chance 49.9–50.0%".
- These hyperparameters were chosen on the same LOSO folds they are scored on, so the new
  numbers carry selection optimism of roughly a point. Print that on chart 1 and chart 2.
- Rep-model numbers are series-level (1,368 points); window numbers are window-level
  (29,871 rows, correlated within a volunteer). Never put both on one axis without saying so.
- `object sizes, not linked flash` applies to anything that is not the 96,164 B link.
