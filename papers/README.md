# Papers

## Included

**`2022-exercise-recognition-and-evaluation-ESWA-arXiv2401.12857.pdf`**
García-de-Villa, S., Casillas-Pérez, D., Jiménez-Martín, A., García-Domínguez, J. J.
"Simultaneous exercise recognition and evaluation in prescribed routines: Approach to virtual
coaches." *Expert Systems with Applications* 199:116990 (2022).
Preprint: arXiv:2401.12857 · doi:10.1016/j.eswa.2022.116990
Licence: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (redistributable with attribution, non-commercial, unmodified).

This is the paper whose method we are compared against, and the one that produced the dataset's
benchmark numbers.

## Not included, deliberately

**The PHYTMO data descriptor** — García-de-Villa et al., *Scientific Data* 9:266 (2022),
doi:10.1038/s41597-022-01387-2, open access CC BY 4.0. Available at
<https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01387-2>. The publisher blocks scripted downloads, so a
link is stored rather than a copy. It is the paper that defines the dataset: columns, sampling rate,
test protocol, and what "correctly performed" means for each exercise.

## Why the NC-ND licence matters here

The preprint is CC BY-NC-ND: no derivatives, no commercial use. Quoting it and shipping the
unmodified PDF in a course repository is fine. Cutting figures out of it and pasting them into our
own slides or a poster would be creating a derivative — attribute it and link instead.
